Version: 0.12.0
Date: 2026-11-01
  Features:
    - Follows fluids: a crafted fluid takes the shortest pipe route (through tanks and pumps) to the next crafter that uses it, a rail station pump or a consumer, and cam glides along that route at setting "Fluid speed". At a station pump cam waits for a fluid wagon, rides along and continues from the pump that empties it at the other end
    - Added setting "Fluid speed", express belt speed by default
    - While cam follows a fluid, the pipe network lights up with dashed, round-capped lines in the fluid's color like the game's pipeline visualization, the route brighter (setting "Highlight fluid networks"), since the game's own pipeline visualization can't be turned on by script
    - Added setting "Skip ahead on full belts" (on by default). Off: cam stays with its own item on a full belt instead of jumping to another piece, skipping ahead out of buildings stays on
    - Follows robot items put into a roboport: cam rides along with the next robot of that kind that leaves it
    - Follows food put into a Pyanodons dig site: cam rides along with the digosaur it sends off and comes back with its ore
  Scripting:
    - Watchdogs that skip ahead handed over to carry field skipped ("belt", "inserter" or "building")
    - Scripted focuses can set prefer_products, fluid_prefer, fluid_speed and skip_glide_speed (camera glides to a skip-ahead target at a steady speed)
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed following the wrong product when a circuit-controlled crafter switches its recipe as soon as the craft finishes
    - Fixed losing focus when an inserter drops onto a short underground exit of a fast belt
    - Fixed a robot delivering more of the same item to a requester chest being taken for ours

Version: 0.11.0
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - Added setting "Skip ahead when stuck" (on by default): on a full belt, cam jumps to the frontmost same item downstream and follows the first same item an inserter picks up from that stretch. If nothing gets taken out of a building (chest, wagon, full crafter output), cam follows an inserter already holding the same item from there. An inserter that can't drop onto a full belt hands over to the frontmost same item on it
    - Added setting "Ticks until item counts as stuck", 60 by default
    - Added setting "Recipes with multiple outputs": first out (old behavior), main product (default), or new item - main product or a product cam hasn't followed yet
    - Follows construction robots that carry the item to a ghost; when the ghost gets built, cam rests on the new building (end of the line)
  Changes:
    - Ported to Factorio 2.1 (underground belts use LuaEntity::underground_belt_neighbour)
    - Replaced __CONTROL_STYLE_BEGIN__ / __CONTROL_STYLE_END__ in locale with rich text, they crash headless 2.1.23 on load
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed remote interface focus_start_from_point not resolving its surface argument
